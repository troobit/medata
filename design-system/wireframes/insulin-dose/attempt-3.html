One slot, two states. from 60 g at 5 g/U holds the caption slot only while the seeded value is untouched; the first press of either step control replaces it with the plain units label, permanently for that presentation. Same font in both states, so the slot cannot change height on the swap. The carbs mode of the same sheet carries the segment on its own second line instead — grams · 12 U.

Why this frame carries no zone marks. Zone names are declared once per surface in design-system/surfaces.md and reused by every option for that surface. No vocabulary is declared for log-sheet, so this frame marks nothing: inventing zone names inside one option is exactly the drift the convention exists to stop.

What a zone table cannot express. Attempt 3’s proposition is not a zone choice — it is “this surface does not carry the readout, and three sheets become one”. That is a surface-level move, and it belongs in the surface-delta table in the owning spec’s requirements.md (consolidate, three ids to one), not in the composition record. The two artifacts are complementary and the limit is worth stating out loud.
