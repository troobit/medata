How to use the thing you are looking at

  • Pick a zone above and the same region of all three options lands beside itself. That turns “attempt 2’s total row against attempt 1’s” into a two-second judgement instead of a memory exercise across two rebuilds.
  • Write the winning zones down in composition.md. A table of zone choices is a complete, unambiguous brief for the next option or for the SwiftUI — and a composed option is an ordinary option: build it as attempt-4.html and it appears here on the same terms as the other three.
  • The zone-choice table outlives these files. When the implementation lands the folder is deleted and the table, with its reasons, moves to the owning spec’s decision_log.md.

What this page does not do

  • It does not settle decomposition. Attempts 1 and 2 render the second line identically in places; one inlined it, the other extracted App/DoseReadoutLine.swift. A picture has no channel for that, and neither does a zone choice.
  • It does not check anything. Nothing verifies that a SwiftUI implementation respects the zone boundaries its wireframe declared; zones are a naming convention, not a construct.
  • It does not replace the on-device gate, and a composed option is still an option, not a decision. It still has to be looked at on the phone.
