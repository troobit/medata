# Depth-Grown Food Region

## Overview

On the 2026-09-24 sitting the bundled segmenter (`coreml_ab812dc3aa9d`) labelled 0.5 % of the frame as bread on a sesame roll that fills 7 % of it, while the LiDAR depth shows the whole roll as one raised slab with a sharp edge (corpus bundle `1790223818017-success`, outcome `72B75CD0`). Volume was integrated over the two specks and the record read 4 g of carbohydrate. This change grows the segmenter's food region into the connected raised depth region around it before volume, so a food the model only partly recognises is still measured whole. It is deterministic geometry on data the single-view LiDAR path already holds; the model is untouched.

## Requirements

1. The system MUST, on the single-view LiDAR path, extend every food-like region of the regularised nadir label map (named, liquid, or `unknown_food`) into the depth pixels reachable from it through 4-neighbour steps whose depth difference is at most a cliff threshold, and label each added pixel with the class of the seed region it was reached from; a pixel reachable from seeds of two classes takes the class reached first in raster order of the seeds.
2. The system MUST add only pixels whose LiDAR confidence meets the volume stage's existing confidence floor and whose surface lies at least a height floor above the current support plane; pixels under the floor, and pixels with no depth, are never added. Seed pixels are kept as labelled whether or not they pass these tests.
3. The system MUST leave the label map unchanged when the grown food-like area exceeds a frame-fraction cap, and MUST record in the outcome measurements whether growth was applied and the food-like pixel count before and after it.
4. The system MUST refit the support plane with the grown food region as the food mask when growth changed the label map, and use the refit plane for volume and for the LiDAR metric scale when the fit succeeds; when the refit refuses, the plane already fitted is kept and the estimate proceeds. The grown region is not re-validated against the refit plane: a grown pixel that sits at the refit plane contributes no height and so no volume.
5. The system MUST integrate volume over every pixel of the grown region, including pixels the segmenter's probability tensor assigns to background, and the review outline and the persisted mask artefact MUST show the grown region.
6. The capture bundle MUST keep the segmenter's own (ungrown) label map, so replay reproduces growth from the same inputs instead of reading its result.
7. The system MUST apply the same growth in the harness single-view replay (`FixtureRunner`) so offline numbers describe the region the app measures and the sweep in Req 8 exercises the shipped rule.
8. The constants (cliff threshold, starting 4 mm; height floor, starting 3 mm; frame-fraction cap, starting 0.35; the confidence floor is `HeightFieldEstimator.tauConfidence`) MUST be settled by a sweep over the corpus captures with depth before the change merges to `main`, with the table in the decision log. Pass rule: on captures whose ungrown food-like area is at least 5 % of the frame, growth adds less than 20 % to that area at the median and the cap trips on none; on `1790223818017-success` the grown region covers the roll's raised slab. The largest cliff value and smallest floor that meet the rule ship.

## Implementation Approach

- **Growth pass.** New `MedataCore/Sources/Volume/FoodRegionGrowth.swift`, a pure function `grow(argmax:depth:intrinsics:supportPlane:palette:config:) -> Result` returning the grown `ArgmaxMap`, a `BinaryMask` of the added pixels, and the before/after counts. Work on the depth grid (256×192): map each colour-grid food-like pixel to its depth pixel the way `sampleConfidenceUInt8` (`VolumeTypes.swift:150`) does, giving each depth cell the label of the first food-like colour pixel in raster order that lands on it; multi-source breadth-first fill from those cells, enqueued in raster order, over 4-neighbours with `|Δz| ≤ cliffMm`, confidence ≥ `tauConfidence`, and height above the plane ≥ `floorMm` computed with the ray–plane intersection already in `HeightFieldEstimator.integrate` (`HeightFieldEstimator.swift:120–140`, lift into a shared helper). First arrival labels a cell, so two seed classes on one slab split it by distance rather than one class taking the slab (Decision 1). Map grown cells back to the colour grid by nearest cell (a 7.5×7.5 block); a colour pixel keeps its existing food-like label if it has one. Deterministic, single-threaded.
- **Silhouette.** `HeightFieldEstimator.Inputs` gains `grownRegion: BinaryMask?` (default nil). The silhouette test `(1 - qBg) < tauSilhouette` (`HeightFieldEstimator.swift:96`) is bypassed for pixels the mask marks. Nothing else in the integrator changes, so ungrown estimates are byte-identical.
- **Pipeline wiring.** In `Pipeline.estimate` (`Pipeline.swift`, `case .singleViewLidar`, ~line 366): after `enforceMinimumFoodCoverage` and before `HeightFieldEstimator.integrate`, run growth; when it changed the map, call `fitSupportPlane` (`Pipeline.swift:655`) again with `PipelineBridges.foodMask(from: grownArgmax, palette:)`, take its plane on success, and recompute `lidarMmPerPx` and the resolved `MetricScale` (stage E, `Pipeline.swift:~283`) from it so the scale and the plane agree. Downstream users of the nadir label map take the grown map: the integrate call, `InterClassOcclusionDetector` (through the integrator), the mask artefact writer (`Pipeline.swift:579`). `CaptureBundleRecorder` keeps `nadirSeg.argmax`. Diagnostics: `PipelineDiagnostics` gains `recordRegionGrowth(applied:capTripped:before:after:refitReference:)` and the outcome JSON carries it; the existing plane fields describe the plane the volume used.
- **Replay.** `HarnessCore/FixtureRunner.swift:364` calls the same growth before `HeightFieldEstimator.integrate`, and its fitter call (`:308`) is repeated with the grown mask, so `HarnessCLI accuracy` and the corpus sweep exercise the rule. The sweep over the corpus captures with depth (`/Users/r/repos/medata-corpus/captures`, 144 bundles) reports, per capture, the food-like fraction before and after growth and whether the cap tripped; that table sets the constants (Req 8).
- **Dependencies.** `SupportPlane` (plane, `BinaryMask`), `Segmentation` (`ArgmaxMap`, `ClassPalette.isVolumetricClass`), the samplers in `VolumeTypes.swift`. Pattern precedent: the cliff-bounded flood fill in `FixtureRunner.fitPlateRegionPlane` (cross-dataset-calibration design, "steep-sided food presents a >5 mm depth cliff") and the deterministic component pass in `PostProcessing.swift:304`.
- **Out of scope.** The two-view path (`VoxelCarveEstimator` carves from per-view probabilities; the 1148 cm³ `unknown_food` row in outcome `FE37458C` is a separate defect, filed in `specs/BACKLOG.md`). Pre-shutter mask and shutter gating. Model retraining. Changing the segmenter's probabilities or the regularisation pass. Growing across a cliff (a stacked or touching second food).

## Risks and Assumptions

- Risk: a gently sloped food (a rice mound) has no cliff at its edge, so the fill leaks onto the plate; on an `edgeBand` plane the whole plate is above the floor | Mitigation: on a `foodSupport` plane the 3 mm floor stops the leak at the plate surface (fit residuals are 1.5–1.8 mm on this sitting); on `edgeBand` only the frame-fraction cap bounds it. The corpus sweep (Req 8) measures how often the cap trips and whether 4 mm cuts real food.
- Risk: the 3 mm floor excludes food thinner than that (a leaf, a smear) from growth | Mitigation: such pixels keep whatever label the segmenter gave them; growth only ever adds.
- Risk: the refit plane with the grown mask lands on the food itself if the grown region under-covers it | Mitigation: the fitter's admissibility rules are unchanged and a refusal keeps the first plane (Req 4); the outcome row records both fits.
- Risk: growth changes every single-view number, so offline figures recorded before it are not comparable | Mitigation: outcome rows carry the applied flag and counts; replay with the cap at 0 reproduces the ungrown estimate.
- Assumption: on this sitting the first fit was `edgeBand` (table) with a 19.7 mm ring median, so "above the plane" includes the plate; only the cliff bounds the fill. The refit with the whole roll as the mask is expected to admit `foodSupport` on the plate top.
- Assumption: the depth grid is 256×192 against a 1920×1440 colour frame (7.5× per axis), so the fill runs over ~49k cells and adds no measurable latency.
- Prerequisite: `make test` green at `365aff0`; the corpus bundles pulled 2026-09-24 (`pulls/20260924-1`).
