# Two-View Trust — Requirements (draft for the 2026-09-25 10:00 decision)

## Introduction

Two-view capture (nadir + oblique photo, voxel carve, scale from LiDAR or an ID-1 card) is the path every phone without LiDAR must use and the path a LiDAR phone takes in Double mode. The night audit (`docs/agent-notes/two-view-geometry-audit.md`) found that no two-view volume in the corpus has ever come from the carve: the stored inter-view transform does not match the photos, so the two silhouettes never intersect and every number is the 30 mm single-view extrusion. On top of that, the segmenter labels an unfamiliar food differently in the two views, so even a working carve would not have run; and the ID-1 card scale path has never executed end to end. The product owner's proposal — the user identifies the food in both photos — is the right lever for the segmenter half, and useless until the geometry half is fixed.

This spec orders the work so each step is verifiable on the phone within a sitting.

## Requirements

### 1. Two-view geometry is verified before anything is built on it

1.1 The system MUST record, for every two-view attempt, the nadir and oblique `camera.transform`, frame timestamps, tracking state and the session run count in the capture bundle, so a stored transform can be audited against the photos offline.
1.2 The inter-view transform applied by the carve MUST be in millimetres and in the camera frame the pixel model uses (u right, v down, −Z forward); the conversion point MUST be one function with a unit test that maps a known 3-D point between two synthetic poses.
1.3 A device verification MUST show, on a printed checkerboard or ID-1 card visible in both views, that the corners back-projected from the nadir land on the oblique corners within 10 px after the transform; the carve is not trusted before this passes.
1.4 Until 1.3 passes, the two-view record MUST carry a degraded flag (the existing `singleViewOnly` σ_view tier is not enough: the carve itself never ran) and the review MUST show it.

### 2. Cross-view reconciliation before the carve

2.1 When a view's food-like pixels form one connected object (the largest connected component holds at least 90 % of them after a small dilation), every class the segmenter scattered over that object MUST be read as one class: the named class with the most pixels, or `unknown_food` when none is named. In two-view capture the two objects MUST then be carved under one class: the nadir's named class wins over the oblique's, a named class wins over `unknown_food`, and the user's choice (Req 3) wins over both. The same single-object rule applies to the single-view path before region growth. The reconciliation MUST be recorded on the outcome row (each view's classes, whether it was a single object, and the chosen class).
2.2 A class present in only one view MUST NOT be extruded from the oblique view alone (kept from bugfix two-view-unknown-carve); extrusion from the nadir alone MUST be flagged degraded on the row.
2.3 On multi-food plates the reconciliation MUST NOT merge regions across a real boundary: it applies only to the single-region case until instance matching exists.

### 3. The user identifies the food in both photos (both modes)

3.0 The measured purpose of the tap is to **anchor the region growth's seed set**, not to narrow the carve's silhouette. The 2026-09-25 synthetic control on `1790318627741` (exact silhouettes, real intrinsics, real baseline, voxelised truth 337 cm³ for a 120 × 70 × 40 mm box) returns 800.7 cm³ at 26°, 832.8 at the real 22.2° inter-view rotation, 712.0 at 40° and 552.0 at 60°: two cones close only once h·tan(θ) exceeds the object's extent along the tilt direction (139 mm / tan 22.2° ≈ 340 mm), so at every tilt the shutter allows (25° ± 15°, `CaptureFlowModel.obliqueTiltOk`) the hull above the food is un-carved. Attribution on the real bundle: grid vertical cap ~92 %, over-wide nadir mask ~7 % (92.6 cm² measured against ~84 cm² real), oblique mask 0–10 cm³. Tighter silhouettes therefore CANNOT halve the two-view over-read; only a height bound can, and that is a separate change. What the tap does earn is the single-view growth leak, where the seed set is every pixel the segmenter called food-like — plate speckles included — which anchors the depth-grown height band to plate height instead of food height.
3.1 The identification MUST be **one tap per view**, and the system MUST NOT offer any other region input: no drag, no box, no pinch-to-size, no lasso, no brush, no outline vertex handle and no boundary nudge (meal-review Decision 4 stands — ~79 s per hand-painted mask, ~4 mm touch error). What the user supplies is a point, not a region; every silhouette is derived from that point by the deterministic on-device passes that already exist (no model inference beyond the segmenter pass already run, no network).
3.2 In single-view mode the tap MUST be taken on the frozen nadir frame after the shutter and before the estimate runs. In Double mode the nadir tap MUST be taken **between the two shots**, on the frozen nadir frame with the pre-shutter food region outlined (`CaptureResult.preShutterFoodMask`), so a nadir with nothing usable is caught before the oblique is spent. The oblique tap MUST be taken after the oblique shutter and before the estimate runs, on the frozen oblique frame.
3.3 The oblique seed MUST be proposed from the nadir seed rather than asked for again whenever the geometry can place it: the nadir seed's point on the support plane is projected into the oblique view through `transform1To2Mm` and the oblique intrinsics (verified to land within 10 px, Decision 4), and when that point falls inside a carvable connected component of the oblique argmax it becomes the oblique seed with source `projectedFromNadir`. The user MUST be able to override it with a tap. When there is no depth and no card plane to place the nadir seed, or the projection lands outside every carvable component, the user MUST be asked for the oblique tap.
3.4 In a view that has a seed, the seed MUST do two things before the carve: (a) every carvable connected component that does not contain the seed MUST be cleared from that view's silhouette, in the argmax and in the probability tensor, by the same mechanism the card exclusion uses (`SegmentationResult.excluding`); and (b) the region growth's seed set MUST be restricted to the surviving component — on a LiDAR phone the depth cells under that component's carvable pixels and no others, feeding the existing floor and seed-height-band rules (depth-grown-food-region Decisions 3–4); on a phone without depth, a colour/edge grow from the seed bounded by that component dilated by a fixed margin. The tap therefore selects and re-seeds in one gesture; there is no separate accept control.
3.5 A tap that falls inside no proposed component MUST still seed: the grow runs from a small fixed neighbourhood of the seed (at least 3 × 3 depth cells — one cell is too noisy to anchor the height band), and the row MUST record that no proposal contained the seed. This is the case where the segmenter found nothing on the food at all.
3.6 A seeded view MUST bypass the Decision 6 connectivity gate: the tap is the object declaration, so the ≥ 90 % largest-component test MUST NOT be applied to it, `applied` is true even for a view holding several objects, and every carvable pixel of the seeded region takes one class — the named carvable class with the most pixels inside that region, else `unknown_food`.
3.7 The class the row carries MUST follow the order: the user's name, then the nadir seeded region's dominant named class, then the oblique's, then `unknown_food` (`ObjectReconciler` already takes `userClass`). A tap alone MUST NOT change the class beyond the dominant class of the region it produced.
3.8 The user MUST be able to name the food once, from the existing relabel shortlist and full list, and the name MUST apply to both silhouettes; naming alone (without a seed) MUST NOT change the geometry, and seeding alone MUST NOT invent a name.
3.9 Declining MUST be a single control on each step and MUST NOT block the estimate: the proposed region stands unchanged, Decision 6's connectivity gate runs exactly as it does today, and the row records `seedSource = none` for that view. The estimate MUST never refuse for want of a tap — a single roll still carves unaided (Decision 6).
3.10 Seeds that disagree between the views MUST refuse the carve rather than carve the mismatch: when the oblique tap falls outside the carvable component containing the projected nadir seed, the two taps do not assert one object, so the carve MUST NOT run, the record falls back to the nadir single-view extrusion flagged degraded (the Req 1.4 flag), and the row MUST record the separation in pixels. The same applies when the nadir seed cannot be projected and the two seeded regions have no overlap under the transform.
3.11 Seeds MUST be stored in sensor-buffer pixel coordinates, as integers, for both views. The frozen frames are displayed rotated to portrait (`RawFrameImage.portraitOrientation`) while the masks, contours and intrinsics are all in the landscape 1920 × 1440 buffer, so the tap MUST be mapped back into buffer space at the moment it is taken and MUST NOT be persisted in view coordinates.
3.12 The outcome row MUST carry a `userRegion` block per view: the seed in buffer pixels, its source (`userTap` / `projectedFromNadir` / `none`), whether it fell inside a proposed component, the carvable pixel count before the seed rule and the silhouette pixel count after growth, the oblique-versus-projected separation in pixels, the seeded region's dominant class, and whether the connectivity gate was overridden. Both confirmed silhouettes MUST be stored per view (`meal_artefacts` already has the per-view key) with provenance `user_confirmed`, and the record MUST show the estimate was user-assisted.
3.13 The outcome row MUST carry the carved hull's vertical extent in millimetres and whether it reached the grid's `verticalExtentMm` cap. This is now an audit field rather than an open question — the cap is measured at ~92 % of the over-read (3.0) and is bounded by a separate change — but every row MUST still say whether the hull ran into the cap, so a regression in that bound is visible without re-running the control.
3.14 The harness MUST replay a tap that no longer has a user by reading it: the fixture carries both seeds and their sources, `FixtureRunner` applies the identical seed rule, and `run(userSeeds: nil)` replays without it. The harness MUST also accept seeds on the command line (`volumes --nadir-seed x,y --oblique-seed x,y`) so bundles captured before this feature can be carved from a hand-placed seed. The same seeds on the same bundle MUST produce the same silhouette byte for byte.
3.15 The offline seed replay MUST run before any capture-surface code is written, and its gate is the **single-view** leak, not the two-view volume: `1790315900185-success.fixture` (growth 89 k → 242 k px) and `1790315865030-success.fixture` (122 k → 236 k px) MUST be replayed from a hand-placed seed and their grown pixel counts and volumes compared with the 266.9–272.0 cm³ the fifth sitting read on the same roll. If one seed does not materially cut the grown region on those two bundles, the tap has no measured benefit and Req 3 MUST be re-decided before any UI is built. The four two-view bundles (851, 920, 926.9, 930 cm³) MUST be replayed in the same pass, but only to record how little the seed moves them (~7 % of the excess is silhouette); they MUST NOT be used as the pass/fail criterion.
3.16 The surface MUST ship as at least two attempts (CLAUDE.md, the device test gate): (A) a per-view tap on the frozen frame immediately after each shutter, as 3.2 describes, and (B) one confirmation surface after both shots showing both frames side by side. Each is committed on a clean tree and tagged `two-view-identify-attempt-N` so `git describe --tags` names the installed one.
3.17 The surface MUST carry no reassurance or disclaimer copy: the frozen frame, the outline, a skip control and the shutter or Estimate control, and nothing else. A well-recognised plate MUST cost at most two taps more than today, and one when the oblique seed is projected.
3.18 The single-view LiDAR path MUST gain the seed: it is where the measured benefit lives (3.0, 3.15). The two-view path MUST keep it too — the tap is the same gesture and the same code on both, and the non-LiDAR path has no other lever — but a two-view number MUST NOT be read as evidence for or against the tap until the grid height bound lands.
3.19 The non-LiDAR two-view path MUST NOT be presented, in copy or in σ, as fixed by the tap: with no depth there is no height bound at any tilt the shutter allows, so the hull stays open above the food and the tap changes only the ~7 % the silhouette owns. Whatever bound replaces the grid cap MUST have a card-plane or two-view-derived equivalent before the non-LiDAR path is called trustworthy.

### 4. The ID-1 card path is honest and verifiable

4.1 `scaleSource` MUST say `card` only when a card was actually used for scale, and a detected rectangle whose scale disagrees with LiDAR by more than a bound MUST NOT raise σ_scale.
4.2 The card's PnP residual and derived distance MUST be persisted on the outcome row.
4.3 Double mode MUST show the "include an ID-1 card" reminder that iphone-experience Req 6.1 promised, and on a phone without LiDAR the shutter MUST NOT arm until a card is detected live.
4.4 `CardOnlyPlaneFitter` MUST be fed the food's lower silhouette edges (pipeline Req 4.3), not the card's corners and a constant; until then the card-only path MUST refuse rather than return an invented plane.
4.5 A Debug switch MUST let a LiDAR phone run the non-LiDAR path (depth cleared, card branch taken) so the card path is verifiable on the phones in hand; the harness MUST be able to replay it.
4.6 A detected card MUST NOT contribute food pixels: its solved image quadrilateral (nadir) and its projection into the oblique view (via the Req 1 transform and the card plane) MUST be cleared to background in both argmax maps before region growth and volume, and the outcome row MUST record the pixel count removed. Evidence: 2026-08-11 capture `1786439234576`, bank card integrated as `cheese` 43.7 g.

## Out of scope

Instance segmentation; spatial matching of multiple foods across views; brush or lasso editing; any change to the single-view LiDAR path; model retraining.
